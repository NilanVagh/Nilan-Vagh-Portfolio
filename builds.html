01Flagship build

Calibration Engine

A clearer way to run performance calibration.

The messy work

Calibration brings together performance data, manager judgment, promotion decisions and fairness checks — usually under time pressure.

What this makes easier

Managers arrive prepared, leaders see the right context, promotion guardrails are visible, and the decision record is not lost after the meeting.

What sits underneath

Role-based views, async prep, promotion checks and an audit trail that shows what changed and why.

What it connects to

Lattice, BambooHR, Monday.com and People Team calibration rules. The prototype shows how those inputs could come together in one workflow.

Where AI could help later

AI could summarise review evidence or flag inconsistencies, but ratings, promotion calls and final sign-off stay human-owned.

Rem Review Planning Studio

A clearer way to plan annual remuneration decisions.

The messy work

Rem review means balancing budgets, performance, fairness, salary bands and exceptions at the same time — often across too many spreadsheets.

What this makes easier

Total Rewards and People leaders can test scenarios, understand cohort impact and see exceptions before decisions move downstream.

What sits underneath

Budget envelopes, eligibility checks, cohort modelling and outputs that Finance and leaders can actually review.

What it connects to

BambooHR, Monday.com, Finance inputs and Total Rewards planning rules. It sits upstream of execution systems like ChartHop.

Where AI could help later

AI could explain scenario changes or summarise exceptions, but budget authority and approvals stay with TR, Finance and People leaders.

Role Architecture Studio

A clearer way to assess role level, pay range and internal role changes.

The messy work

Job levelling is rarely just a title problem. It affects pay, fairness, hiring, internal moves and career growth.

What this makes easier

Managers can submit a role or JD and get a structured recommendation, rationale, confidence level and review path.

What sits underneath

Role architecture rules, salary bands, exception thresholds and a decision record — with humans reviewing boundary cases.

What it connects to

Job architecture tables, salary bands, prior role decisions and a curated JD library. GAS, Sheets and Drive are a realistic v1 build path.

Where AI could help later

AI could help extract evidence from approved JDs and prior decisions. The level rules, range guardrails and approvals stay outside the model.

Workforce Decision Intelligence

A clearer way to see where workforce action may be needed.

The messy work

Workforce data is usually split across systems. Leaders get charts, but not always a clear view of what needs attention now.

What this makes easier

Movement, performance, engagement, pay position and retention risk are brought into one signal view so leaders can ask better questions faster.

What sits underneath

Signal logic, division-aware views and action prompts. The tool surfaces issues; leaders and People Partners decide what to do.

What it connects to

BambooHR, Lattice performance, engagement data and salary-positioning context. The demo shows how those signals could sit together.

Where AI could help later

AI could generate leader briefs or help investigate signal drivers, but action ownership remains with leaders and People Partners.

EOR Entity Readiness Matrix

A clearer way to decide when EOR is no longer the right operating model.

The messy work

EOR-to-entity decisions cut across People, Finance, Legal and Business. Without a shared framework, each team sees a different version of the risk.

What this makes easier

Each function owns its input, the overall readiness view is shared, and high-risk triggers cannot be averaged away.

What sits underneath

Weighted triggers, hard overrides, stakeholder-owned ratings and an audit trail for a multi-function decision.

What it connects to

Deel headcount and cost data, Finance / Tax inputs, Legal risk ratings and Business market commitment signals.

Where AI could help later

AI could summarise risk notes or prep quarterly review packs. It should not override Finance, Legal or Business ownership.
