01Flagship build

Calibration Engine

Performance calibration as a governed decision workflow.

Problem

Calibration breaks when ratings, promotion recommendations, manager prep, divisional views and executive approval are coordinated through meetings and spreadsheets.

What it proves

Async triage, role-based visibility, promotion guardrails and audit trail can make the decision state visible before the live session.

Governance principle

Do not rely on meeting memory. Make the decision state persistent, permissioned and auditable.

Connected systems

Lattice for performance evidence, BambooHR for employee and manager context, Monday.com for PIP / warning flags, People Team inputs for distribution and promotion guardrails.

Future AI layer

AI could summarise review evidence or surface inconsistency signals, but ratings, promotion calls and final sign-off stay human-owned.

Rem Review Planning Studio

Budget modelling and decision-support for annual remuneration review.

Problem

Rem review decisions happen upstream in spreadsheets, while governance only appears downstream when outcomes are being executed.

What it proves

Budget envelopes, eligibility rules, cohort modelling and governance-ready outputs can be built into the planning layer.

Governance principle

Put the guardrails where the decision is made, not after it has already been distributed.

Connected systems

BambooHR and Monday.com extracts feed eligibility and employee context; Finance and Total Rewards inputs define budget envelopes, cohort rules and merit assumptions.

Future AI layer

AI could explain scenario movements and summarise exceptions; budget authority, eligibility rules and approvals remain with TR, Finance and People leaders.

Role Architecture Studio

Structured role assessment, hiring-range guidance and internal-change review.

Problem

Role and level decisions are often treated like writing exercises, even though they have compensation, equity and governance consequences.

What it proves

Deterministic architecture rules, exception routing and a decision certificate can support managers without pretending the tool owns the judgment.

Governance principle

AI may assist evidence extraction later. The level rules, exception thresholds and approvals stay outside the model.

Connected systems

Designed for job architecture tables, salary bands, prior role decisions and a curated JD library. GAS / Sheets / Drive is a realistic v1 build path.

Future AI layer

AI can assist evidence extraction from approved JDs and prior decisions. Architecture thresholds, range governance and review routing stay deterministic.

Signal + governance tools

Decision surfaces for workforce visibility and global operating risk.

04Live demo

Workforce Decision Intelligence

Decision-ready visibility for workforce leaders.

Problem

Most workforce dashboards report what changed. They do not show whether a specific workforce issue now requires intervention, and why.

What it proves

Movement, performance, engagement, pay position and retention risk can be combined into a prioritised signal layer.

Governance principle

Signals should prompt decisions; they should not automate them.

Connected systems

BambooHR, Lattice performance, Lattice engagement and salary-positioning context are joined into a decision-ready signal layer.

Future AI layer

AI could generate leader briefs or investigate signal drivers, but the threshold logic and action ownership remain governed.

05Live demo

EOR Entity Readiness Matrix

When to exit EOR and establish a legal entity — governed, weighted and auditable.

Problem

EOR-to-entity decisions span People, Finance, Legal and Business, but without a shared framework each team applies its own lens.

What it proves

Weighted triggers, hard overrides, stakeholder-owned inputs and audit trail can make a multi-function decision defensible.

Governance principle

People can architect the system without owning every domain input.

Connected systems

Deel headcount and EOR cost data combine with Finance / Tax, Legal and Business-owned qualitative ratings.

Future AI layer

AI could summarise market risk notes or prep quarterly review packs. It should not override Finance, Legal or Business ownership of risk inputs.

Maturity model

Honest by design: demo, prototype and production are different things.

Each build is deliberately labelled so the portfolio does not overclaim. A live HTML prototype can prove the workflow logic. Production requires source-of-truth data, permissions, integrations, audit reliability and operating ownership.
