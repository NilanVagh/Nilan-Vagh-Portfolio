The problem
Onyx operates in 14 countries. 12 are on EOR. No repeatable system existed to assess when entity setup was legally required, financially justified, or strategically necessary.
The design principle
People Team cannot own this in a silo. Finance owns Tax PE risk. Legal owns compliance triggers. Business owns strategic commitment. People Team architects the system — domain experts own the inputs.
The output
A weighted composite score across 7 drivers. RAG status drives action. RED triggers an automatic entity setup workstream. Every input change is logged with actor, role, and reason.
⚠
Hard Override Rule: Tax PE / Business Activity Risk = 3 (High) automatically triggers RED status — regardless of composite score. You cannot average away existential legal risk. If local entity is legally required (visas, local invoicing, authority presence), the matrix forces escalation.
