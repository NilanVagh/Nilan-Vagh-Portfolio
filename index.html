The category

People Infrastructure

People Infrastructure is the layer that sits between workforce intent and workforce outcome. Not the policies, not the systems, not the headcount — the governed logic that routes decisions to the right people, at the right moment, with the right guardrails.

Most People functions have the systems. Few have the infrastructure. The difference is whether the work is mainly about updating a record — or mainly about helping the right people make the right decision in the right order.
