Operating philosophy

AI is only useful when the operating layer underneath is clear.

AI can accelerate the experience. It should not own the judgment. The work starts with the infrastructure: source of truth, process ownership, decision rules, approvals and the human review points that keep consequential work accountable.

How I build

From messy People work to durable operating infrastructure.

01

Map the decision path

What is the user trying to do, who owns the call, and where does the work break today?

02

Define rules and exceptions

Eligibility, thresholds, approvals, guardrails and where the system must stop.

03

Design workflow state

Make progress, ownership, routing and auditability visible by default.

04

Add AI selectively

Use AI to assist retrieval, summarisation or evidence extraction — not to own consequential judgment.

05

Prototype, test, iterate

Build just enough to make the operating model tangible, then harden what proves useful.
