Decision guardrails

The architecture assesses the work, not the person, title or tenure.

Titles are labels
Title wording can prompt a consistency check; scope, accountability and decision rights determine level.
Performance and level are distinct
Strong performance may inform readiness, but it does not automatically change the architecture level of a role.
Consequential cases stay human-led
Boundary cases, L5/L6 recommendations and range exceptions route to People or Total Rewards review.
Current operating model
Structured evidence + architecture rules + exception routing. Deterministic by design.
Future-ready, not AI-dependent.
Future versions may connect a Google Drive JD library, prior Onyx role decisions, architecture tables and salary bands, with optional AI-assisted evidence extraction. Governance logic and approval accountability remain outside the model.

How this demo works

Role Architecture Studio is a governed decision workflow. It structures submitted evidence, maps work against transparent architecture rules, recommends a level and hiring range, routes exceptions to people, and creates a reusable decision record.

  1. Select Guided role summary and load the Senior Software Engineer example.
  2. Generate the structured recommendation and inspect factor evidence plus adjacent-level reasoning.
  3. Open Hiring Range and switch the candidate profile.
  4. Open Internal Role Review, load Lea Morgan and assess the promotion.
  5. Submit the recommendation for review and inspect the audit log and certificate.

Future iterations could add optional AI-assisted evidence extraction from approved sources. The architecture rules, exception routing and human approvals remain the governing layer.
