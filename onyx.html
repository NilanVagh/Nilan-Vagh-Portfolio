The Sandbox Environment

Onyx

Every tool in this portfolio was built for Onyx. Not for a client. Not for a case study. For a fictional company I designed specifically to be the right data environment for the tools I wanted to build.

"Onyx is the fictional company I designed these tools for. It mirrors the environment my target market operates in — AI-native, scaling fast, ANZ + US footprint, complex workforce decisions. Every tool you'll see was built as if Onyx's People team needed it: real governance logic, real workflow architecture, real decision support. The data is dummy. The thinking is not."

Why Onyx exists

Every demo uses the same fictional company so the tools can be tested against one consistent workforce environment: the same levels, salary bands, divisions, regions and governance context.

What is synthetic: employees, salaries, leaders, workforce data and company details.

What is real: the workflow design, decision logic, governance model and build thinking.

Why a fictional company?

Portfolio demonstrations of People tools have a problem: the data has to be fake either way, but the context usually doesn't exist. Without context — without knowing what kind of company uses this, at what scale, with what workforce complexity — the tool is a feature showcase. The thinking behind it is invisible.

Onyx solves that. It gives every tool a consistent operating environment: the same headcount, job levels, salary bands, leadership team and divisions. When you see the Calibration Engine, you know the population it governs. When you open the Role Architecture Studio, you see the same architecture and salary-band context applied to a specific role decision. The tools read as a connected product suite rather than a collection of demos.

The specific choice of Onyx — a 260-person AI-native SaaS company with 235 permanent FTE + 25 EOR across five markets — is deliberate. It is the kind of environment where the People Infrastructure problems I am solving become most visible: fast-growing, technically sophisticated, operating across geographies, with workforce complexity that outpaces standard HR tooling. That is not accidental. It is the audience.

People Tech Stack — Why the tools exist

Like most companies at Series C, Onyx runs a fragmented stack of best-of-breed tools that each solve one problem — none of which integrate cleanly. The People team is the integration layer. That's not a failure of vendor selection. It's the operating reality for companies that scaled fast and bought tools as needs emerged.

The tools in this portfolio exist because this stack creates real decision-infrastructure gaps that no enterprise HR platform fills out of the box — governance logic, context-specific workflows, and decision infrastructure have to be built, not configured.

BambooHR
Core HRIS
System of record for employee data, org structure, leave, and basic reporting. Excellent at storing what happened. No workflow logic.
Lattice
Performance & Engagement
Goal-setting, performance reviews, 1:1s, and engagement surveys. Holds final ratings. Cannot support calibration workflows, distribution guardrails, or promotion routing.
Deel
EOR & Contractor Management
Manages EOR employment across five markets (UK, Singapore, Germany, Canada, Netherlands). Handles local compliance, payroll, and contracts. No decision logic for when to exit EOR and establish a legal entity.
Monday.com
Workflow & Process Tracking
Tracks operational workflows including PIP cases, formal warnings, parental leave, and crossboarding. The process layer BambooHR doesn't support natively — where case management and exception tracking actually lives.
Google Workspace
Productivity suite & build layer
Sheets, Docs, Drive, Gmail, and Forms — the standard suite every People team runs on. Google Apps Script sits on top of this as the build layer: the tool that lets you build governed workflows and decision-support systems without engineering support.
Slack + Email
Informal process layer
Where approvals actually happen. Where context lives. Where decisions get made and then recorded elsewhere. The gap between this and the system of record is the infrastructure problem.
Global Workforce — EOR Hiring Map

Onyx operates legal entities in Australia and the United States. Five additional markets run on EOR through Deel — a deliberate choice to hire ahead of entity setup in strategic markets. The UK is approaching entity threshold — headcount is at 10, Tax PE risk is rising, and the break-even on entity setup is under 18 months. The EOR Entity Readiness Matrix was built to govern exactly this decision — replacing fragmented Finance / Legal / People conversations with a weighted, auditable framework that nobody can override without a logged reason.
